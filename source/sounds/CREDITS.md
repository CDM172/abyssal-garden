# Sound credits

All clips are **CC0 (public domain)** from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org). They were trimmed, converted to mono
and compressed to small MP3 files, which `build.mjs` embeds in `index.html`, so the game stays one file.

| File | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `heart.mp3` | Heartbeat when a shark is close or hunting you | [Heartbeat (single sound)](https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-single-sound), submitted by qubodup |
| `bite.mp3` | A shark bite | [Crunchy bite](https://opengameart.org/content/crunchy-bite) by fvcalderan |
| `splash.mp3` | Breaking the surface and going back under | [6 Short water splashes](https://opengameart.org/content/6-short-water-splashes) by ezwa |
| `breath.mp3` | Catching your breath at the surface | [Breathing Tired](https://opengameart.org/content/breathing-tired) by mikeask |
| `bubbles.mp3` | Breathing out under water | [Bubble Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/bubble-sound-effects) by bmaczero |
| `wave.mp3` | Waves while you are at the surface | [Beach Ocean Waves](https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves) by jasinski, submitted by qubodup |
